Instagram Hashtags: How Many to Use and How to Pick Them

Updated November 4, 2026

Hashtags used to be a growth trick. Today they are more of a labeling tool. They help Instagram understand what your post is about, and they help people searching a topic find it. Here is how to use them well.

How many should you use?

Instagram's guidance on hashtags has changed over the years, and the app has moved toward encouraging fewer of them. A small number of highly relevant hashtags is better than a long list of loosely related ones. Check the current limit and advice in the app, and as a general rule start with three to five that truly describe your post.

Relevance beats popularity

A hashtag with millions of posts moves fast, and your post disappears within seconds. A smaller, more specific hashtag gives you a better chance of being seen by people who care about that exact topic.

  • Broad: #fitness (very competitive)
  • Medium: #homeworkouts
  • Specific: #fifteenminutehomeworkout

Mix them, with more weight on the specific ones.

How to find good hashtags

  1. Type a topic into the Instagram search bar and open the Tags tab.
  2. Look at the suggestions and the number of posts for each.
  3. Open a few and check that the top posts match your content.
  4. Look at what similar creators in your niche use.

Where to put them

You can put hashtags in the caption or in the first comment. Instagram has said both work the same. Putting them at the end of the caption, separated by a line break, keeps your writing clean.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Irrelevant hashtags. Using a trending tag that has nothing to do with your post misleads people and can reduce trust.
  • Banned or restricted hashtags. Some tags are limited because of misuse. Check a tag by searching it before you use it.
  • Copying the same block every time. Tailor the tags to each post.
  • Stuffing too many. A wall of tags looks spammy.

Create a branded hashtag

A short, unique hashtag tied to your name or project helps you collect your own content in one place. It also gives followers an easy way to share their posts with you.

Keep an eye on the results

In your post insights, check how many people found you through hashtags. If the number is small, try different tags. If it is high, note which tags helped and reuse them.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
