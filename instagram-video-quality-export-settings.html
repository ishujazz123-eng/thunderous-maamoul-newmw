Why Your Instagram Videos Look Blurry, and How to Fix It

Updated November 4, 2026

You filmed a beautiful video, but once it is on Instagram it looks soft and blurry. This is usually not because of Instagram alone. Several small choices affect quality. Here are the main ones.

Use the right aspect ratio

Reels and Stories are designed for a 9:16 vertical format. If you upload a horizontal video, Instagram adds black bars or crops it, and your image shrinks. Film vertically from the start.

Use the right resolution

The standard size for vertical videos is 1080 by 1920 pixels. Uploading lower resolution makes the video look soft when scaled up on a big phone screen. Higher resolution is usually reduced, so there is little benefit beyond this size for Instagram.

Check your camera settings

  • Record at 1080p or higher on your phone.
  • A frame rate of 30 frames per second works well for most content.
  • Turn on stabilization if your phone offers it.

Light is more important than pixels

Dim light forces the camera to add noise and lose detail. Film near a window or use a soft light. Even a cheap phone looks better in good light than an expensive camera in a dark room.

Clean the lens

Fingerprints are one of the most common causes of soft video. Wipe the lens with a clean cloth before you start.

Avoid repeated re-compression

Every time a video is exported, saved, or shared through messaging apps, it is compressed again and loses quality. Edit from the original files and export once.

Upload with a stable connection

A weak connection can cause Instagram to upload a lower-quality version. Use Wi-Fi when possible. In the app settings, look for an option for uploading at the highest quality.

Do not use videos from other apps with watermarks

Videos with watermarks and low resolution from other platforms look worse and may be shown less often. Use original footage whenever you can.

Quick checklist

  1. Film vertically in good light.
  2. Clean your lens.
  3. Edit from original files.
  4. Export once, at 1080 by 1920.
  5. Upload on a strong connection.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
