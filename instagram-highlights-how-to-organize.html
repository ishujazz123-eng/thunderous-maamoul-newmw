Instagram Highlights: How to Organize Your Profile for New Visitors

Updated November 4, 2026

Highlights are the circles under your bio. They keep your best Stories on your profile beyond 24 hours. Used well, they work like a mini website: they help new visitors understand who you are in seconds.

Why Highlights matter

Many people decide whether to follow you after looking at your bio, a few posts, and your Highlights. A tidy set of Highlights shows that your account is active and organized.

How to create a Highlight

  1. Post a Story, or open your Story archive.
  2. Tap the Highlight icon on a Story, or tap the plus sign on your profile.
  3. Select the Stories you want to include.
  4. Give it a short name and choose a cover.

Ideas for Highlight categories

  • Start here: a short introduction to you or your account.
  • Tips or guides: your best educational Stories.
  • Reviews or feedback: kind messages from customers or followers.
  • Behind the scenes: how you work or make things.
  • FAQ: answers to questions you get often.
  • Events or trips: memorable moments worth keeping.

Keep names short

Only about the first twelve characters of a name are shown under the circle. Choose clear one- or two-word names, such as "Tips", "FAQ", or "Travel".

Design simple covers

Covers do not need to be fancy. Use a simple icon or a plain color with a consistent style. You can use a free design app, or even a photo from the Highlight itself.

Order matters

Highlights appear in the order you set. Put the most useful one first, usually an introduction. You can reorder them by pressing and dragging.

Keep them fresh

  • Remove old Stories that are no longer true.
  • Add new ones to the right Highlight when you post.
  • Review your Highlights every few months.

Keep the number manageable

Four to six Highlights is plenty for most accounts. Too many make your profile look cluttered, and visitors will not scroll through all of them.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
