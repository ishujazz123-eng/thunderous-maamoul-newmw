Instagram Collab Posts and Remix: A Beginner's Guide to Working With Other Creators

Updated November 4, 2026

Collaboration is one of the fastest ways to reach new people. Instagram has two main tools for it: Collab posts, where two accounts share one post, and Remix, where you create a Reel in response to another one. Here is how to use both well.

What is a Collab post?

A Collab post appears on both creators' profiles and shares one set of views, likes, and comments. When you create a post or Reel, you can invite another account as a collaborator. They accept the invitation, and the post appears for both audiences.

How to create one

  1. Start a new post or Reel as usual.
  2. On the final screen, choose the option to tag people and select "Invite collaborator".
  3. Pick the account you want to work with.
  4. Share the post. The post is shown on both profiles once they accept.

What is Remix?

Remix lets you record a Reel that plays side by side with someone else's Reel. People use it to react, add commentary, answer a question, or show their version of an idea. You can only remix Reels whose creators have left the feature turned on.

When to use each one

  • Collab post: when you are working on content together, such as a joint tutorial or announcement.
  • Remix: when you want to respond to or build on someone's existing Reel.

Finding the right creators

  • Look for accounts with a similar audience size, so the benefit goes both ways.
  • Choose creators whose topics complement yours rather than compete with them.
  • Follow and engage with their content before you reach out.

How to ask

Send a short, specific message. Say what you admire about their work, explain your idea, and describe what is in it for them. Keep it friendly and make it easy for them to say no.

Ideas for collaborations

  • Swap tips on a shared topic
  • Do a challenge together
  • Interview each other in a Reel
  • Combine skills, for example a cook and a photographer

Tips for success

  1. Agree on who posts and when.
  2. Check the final post together before sharing.
  3. Give credit and reply to comments from both audiences.

Done well, a single collaboration can bring more followers than weeks of posting alone.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
