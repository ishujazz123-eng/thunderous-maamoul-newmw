How to Repost Instagram Content Properly: Permission, Credit and Etiquette

Updated November 4, 2026

Sharing other people's work can build relationships, bring variety to your feed, and support creators you admire. Done carelessly, it can upset people and cause copyright trouble. Here is how to do it respectfully.

Start with permission

Always ask before you repost to your feed. Send a short, friendly message that explains what you want to share and where. For example: "Hi! I love this post and would like to share it on my account with full credit to you. Is that okay?" Keep the reply in case you need it later.

Use built-in sharing tools

  • Share to Story: Tap the paper-plane icon on a post or Reel and add it to your Story. The original account stays tagged.
  • Collab posts: If you work with the creator, invite them to be a collaborator so the post appears on both profiles.
  • Tags and mentions: Tag the creator in your caption or Story when you talk about their work.

Give clear credit

Credit the creator in the caption and tag their account. Be specific: "Photo by @username" is clearer than a vague "via". If the creator asks for a different type of credit, follow their wishes.

Do not alter the content without approval

Do not crop out logos or watermarks, change the message, or add your own branding to someone else's work. This can misrepresent the creator and damage trust.

Be careful with commercial use

If a post is promoting your product or business, you need more than casual permission. Agree in writing on how the content will be used, for how long, and whether any payment applies.

Respect removal requests

If a creator asks you to take their content down, do so quickly and politely. Arguing hurts your reputation far more than losing one post.

Build a system for user-generated content

  1. Create a branded hashtag and invite followers to use it.
  2. Tell people in your bio or Stories that you may feature posts that use it.
  3. Ask permission each time before you feature a post.
  4. Keep a note of who agreed and when.

Why it matters

Creators notice how you treat their work. Respectful sharing often leads to friendships, collaborations, and loyal followers. Careless reposting can lead to complaints, takedowns, and a damaged name.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
