DMCA / Copyright Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ReelSave respects the intellectual property rights of others. We do not host, store, or upload any Instagram content on our servers. The tool only retrieves publicly accessible media at a user's request. If you believe your copyrighted work is being accessed through our service in a way that infringes your rights, please notify us.

Submitting a Notice

Send a written notice through our Contact page (subject: "DMCA Notice") that includes:

  1. Your full name and contact details (email, address, phone)
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed
  3. The specific URL(s) of the material you want addressed
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf
  6. Your physical or electronic signature

What Happens Next

We review valid notices promptly and may block the relevant links or content from being processed by the tool. We may also remove or disable access for users who repeatedly infringe.

Removing Content at the Source

Because the media is hosted by Instagram, the most effective way to remove content is to report it directly to Instagram or Meta through their own copyright reporting process.

Counter-Notice

If you believe a notice was submitted in error, you may send a counter-notice through the same Contact page with your details, the affected URL, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was a mistake.
