Understanding Instagram Insights: The Numbers That Actually Matter

Updated November 4, 2026

Instagram Insights is a free tool for professional accounts that shows how your content performs. It can feel like too many numbers, but only a handful matter. This guide explains what they mean and how to use them.

How to get Insights

Switch your account to a creator or business account in settings. It is free, and it unlocks Insights on your profile and on each post. Note that data starts from the time you switch.

Reach

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your content. It tells you how far a post traveled. Look at how much of your reach came from people who do not follow you, as that shows your discovery power.

Views or impressions

This counts how many times your content was shown, including repeat views. Comparing it to reach shows how often people watched more than once.

Likes, comments, shares and saves

  • Likes are a light signal of approval.
  • Comments show conversation.
  • Shares show that people thought the post was worth sending to someone else.
  • Saves show lasting value. They are a strong signal for educational posts.

Watch time for Reels

For Reels, average watch time and the percentage of people who watched to the end tell you whether your video holds attention. If viewers drop in the first seconds, work on your opening.

Audience insights

Your audience section shows age ranges, locations, and the hours your followers are most active. Use this to adjust the timing of your posts and the topics you cover.

Profile activity

Profile visits and link taps show how many people moved from your content to learning more about you. If visits are high but follows are low, improve your bio or pinned posts.

A simple monthly review

  1. Pick your top three posts by reach and your bottom three.
  2. Write down what the top ones have in common.
  3. Write down what the bottom ones have in common.
  4. Plan your next month to include more of the first and less of the second.

Do not obsess

Numbers move up and down. Look at trends over weeks, not single days. Insights are there to guide your decisions, not to make you anxious.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
