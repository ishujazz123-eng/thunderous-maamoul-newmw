How to Save and Back Up Your Own Instagram Reels, Photos and Stories

Updated November 4, 2026

Your Instagram posts may be some of your most important memories or business assets. Yet many people never back them up. If your account is hacked or deleted, those posts could be gone. Here are the official ways to keep your own content safe.

Save to your camera roll before posting

Instagram can automatically save a copy of your posts, Stories, and Reels to your phone. In the app, open settings, find the sharing or archiving options, and turn on saving of original photos and videos. This is the easiest backup because it happens without you thinking about it.

Use the Story archive

Stories disappear after 24 hours, but Instagram keeps them in your private archive if the archive option is on. Open the archive from your profile menu to look at older Stories and add them to Highlights.

Save an existing Reel or video

For videos you have already posted, check the three-dot menu on the post. Instagram offers options for saving or downloading your own content, and these features can change over time, so look for the current options in the app.

Download your full account data

Instagram lets you request a copy of everything you have shared. In your account settings, look for the option to download your information. You can choose which data to include, the date range, and the file format. Instagram prepares the file and sends a link to your email, so it can take some time.

Store backups in more than one place

  • Your phone's gallery
  • A cloud storage service
  • An external hard drive for important work

Having copies in at least two places protects you if one fails.

Keep the original files

The best quality always comes from the original file on your phone or computer. Instagram compresses media when you upload it, so a copy downloaded later may be lower quality than the one you recorded.

Make it a habit

  1. Turn on automatic saving of originals.
  2. Request a full data download every few months.
  3. Store the file somewhere safe and note the date.

Content from other people

Everything above is about your own content. For other creators' posts, respect their rights and ask for permission before saving or using their work beyond personal viewing within the app's own tools.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
