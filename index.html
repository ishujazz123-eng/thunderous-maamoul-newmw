How to Download Instagram Stories Before They Disappear

Instagram Stories disappear after 24 hours, making it frustrating when you want to save a story you loved but forgot to screenshot or screen record in time. ReelSave solves this problem by letting you download any public Instagram Story directly to your device.

Why Do Instagram Stories Disappear?

Instagram designed Stories to be temporary by nature — they last only 24 hours before being automatically deleted from the platform. This creates urgency but also frustration for users who want to keep content they enjoyed.

How to Save Instagram Stories Using ReelSave

  • Open Instagram and go to the Story you want to save
  • Tap the Share button (arrow icon) at the bottom of the story
  • Tap "Copy Link" to copy the story URL
  • Open ReelSave and select the "Stories" tab
  • Paste the URL and tap Download
  • Your story downloads as an MP4 or JPG instantly

Important Notes About Story Downloads

ReelSave can only download Stories from public accounts. Private account stories require the account owner's permission. Always respect content creators' rights when saving and sharing their stories.

Top 10 Instagram Reels Tips to Grow Your Account in 2026

Instagram Reels remain one of the best ways to grow your Instagram following in 2026. The algorithm heavily favors Reels content, often pushing it to users who don't even follow you yet. Here are the top 10 tips to make your Reels go viral.

1. Hook Viewers in the First 2 Seconds

The first two seconds of your Reel determine whether someone keeps watching or scrolls away. Start with something surprising, funny, or visually striking to instantly grab attention.

2. Use Trending Audio

Instagram prioritizes Reels that use trending sounds and music. Check the Explore page regularly to identify trending audio and incorporate it into your content.

3. Keep It Short and Punchy

While Reels can be up to 90 seconds, the best performing ones are usually under 30 seconds. Get to the point quickly and leave viewers wanting more.

4. Add Text and Captions

Many users watch Reels without sound. Adding text overlays ensures your message gets across even when audio is muted.

5. Post Consistently

The Instagram algorithm rewards consistent creators. Aim to post at least 3-5 Reels per week to maintain momentum and grow your audience steadily.
