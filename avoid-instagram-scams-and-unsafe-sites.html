How to Avoid Instagram Scams and Unsafe Third-Party Sites

Updated November 4, 2026

Scams on Instagram are common, and they often look friendly. Fake giveaways, unsafe websites, and messages that claim to be from Instagram can all lead to a stolen account. Knowing the warning signs helps you stay safe.

Common scams

  • Fake giveaways. An account asks you to follow, click a link, and enter details to claim a prize.
  • Fake support messages. A message says your account will be disabled unless you "verify" through a link.
  • Verification badge offers. Someone offers to get you verified for a fee.
  • Brand collaboration traps. A "brand" asks you to pay for a product or sign up before a promised deal.
  • Phishing links. A link leads to a copy of the Instagram login page designed to steal your password.

Warning signs of an unsafe website or app

  • It asks for your Instagram username and password.
  • It makes you install an unknown app or browser extension.
  • It shows many pop-ups or redirects you to other sites.
  • It asks you to complete surveys or share the link before continuing.
  • It has no privacy policy or contact information.

As a rule, never type your Instagram password into any site that is not instagram.com or the official app.

Protect your account

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication in your account's security settings, preferably with an authenticator app.
  2. Use a strong, unique password that you do not use anywhere else.
  3. Check logged-in devices from time to time and log out of any you do not recognize.
  4. Review connected apps and remove anything you no longer use.

What to do if something looks suspicious

  • Do not click the link, and do not reply.
  • Check the sender's username and profile. Fake accounts often have recent creation dates and few posts.
  • Report the message or account in the app.
  • Instagram sends official security emails only from its official domains. If in doubt, open the app directly and check your settings.

If you think your account was compromised

Change your password at once, log out of all devices, check your email for changes, and follow Instagram's help steps for hacked accounts. Act quickly, because the first hours matter most.

Stay calm and skeptical

Scammers rely on urgency and excitement. If an offer seems too good to be true, or a message pushes you to act immediately, pause and verify before clicking.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
