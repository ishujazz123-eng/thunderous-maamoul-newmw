What Is the Best Time to Post on Instagram? How to Find Yours

Updated November 4, 2026

Search for the best time to post on Instagram and you will find dozens of charts, each saying something different. That is because the best time depends on who follows you, where they live, and when they use the app. Your own data beats any general rule.

Why general charts are unreliable

Studies of millions of posts can show broad patterns, such as more activity in the evening and at lunchtime. But your followers might be students, night-shift workers, or spread across several time zones. A chart built on everyone's data will not match your audience.

Step 1: Look at when your followers are active

If you have a professional account, open Insights and go to your audience section. Instagram shows the days and hours when your followers are most active. This is your best starting point, because it comes from the people you actually want to reach.

Step 2: Test a few time slots

Pick three time slots, for example morning, lunchtime, and evening. Post similar content at each time over two or three weeks. Try to keep other factors, like format and topic, as similar as you can so the comparison is fair.

Step 3: Compare the results

For each post, note the reach, likes, comments, shares, and saves in the first few hours. A post that gains momentum quickly usually keeps going. Write the numbers in a simple table or spreadsheet.

Step 4: Adjust and repeat

Choose the slots that performed best and use them more often. Then test again every month or two, because audiences change over time.

Reels are different from feed posts

Reels are shown to new people over days, not just to your followers in the first hour. For Reels, posting time matters less than the quality of the video. Still, posting when your followers are active gives the video a stronger first test group.

Other things worth considering

  • Time zones. If your audience is spread across countries, pick a time that overlaps with evenings for most of them.
  • Weekdays and weekends. Some audiences are busier on weekends, others on weekdays.
  • Consistency. Posting at a predictable time trains your audience to look for you.

Do not overthink it

Timing is a small lever. A great post at an average time beats an average post at a perfect time. Use your Insights to avoid the worst hours, then put your energy into the content itself.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
