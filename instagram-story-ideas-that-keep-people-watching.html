25 Instagram Story Ideas That Keep People Watching

Updated November 4, 2026

Stories are the casual side of Instagram. They disappear after 24 hours, so you can be more relaxed and experimental. The challenge is knowing what to post. Here are ideas grouped by purpose, plus tips to make them better.

Behind the scenes

  1. Show your workspace or setup for the day.
  2. Share how you prepare for a post or product.
  3. Film a quick time-lapse of something you are making.
  4. Show a mistake and how you fixed it.
  5. Introduce a person or pet who helps you.

Interactive Stories

  1. Post a poll with two choices.
  2. Use the question sticker to ask "Ask me anything".
  3. Run a quiz with a fun fact.
  4. Use an emoji slider to ask how people feel about something.
  5. Ask followers to vote on your next post idea.

Helpful and educational

  1. Share one quick tip in three slides.
  2. Answer a question you received in your messages.
  3. Post a short how-to with text steps.
  4. Share a book, tool, or app you recommend and why.
  5. Do a "myth versus fact" about your topic.

Personal and relatable

  1. Share your morning routine.
  2. Show what you are reading, watching, or listening to.
  3. Post a goal for the week and report back later.
  4. Share a small win.
  5. Talk honestly about something you are still learning.

Community

  1. Reshare a follower's post, with permission and credit.
  2. Share a post from a creator you admire.
  3. Thank people for reaching a milestone with you.
  4. Run a small giveaway following Instagram's rules.
  5. Do a Q&A takeover with a friend or colleague.

Make Stories better

  • Keep each slide short. Aim for a few seconds of video or a clear single image.
  • Use text sparingly. One line per slide is easier to read.
  • Add a clear beginning. Start with the point, not a long introduction.
  • Do not post too many at once. Five to seven slides is plenty for most days.

Save the best ones

If a Story is useful, add it to a Highlight so new visitors can find it later. Stories are a daily habit, and Highlights turn the best of them into lasting content.

This guide is for general information. Instagram features and rules change, so check the app for the latest options.
